
On Linux, `open()` in user mode calls `sys_open()` within the kernel, while 
on Windows, `NtCreateFile()` in user mode calls `NtCreateFile()` within the kernel. 

Lets understand the significance of this: 
The 'target' routine is what matters, not the various transition maneuvers required to reach it. 
The target is neither the handler function involved in the transition nor 
the service implementation itself, but rather the specific service interface being provided.
 
In this respect, the NT interface makes much more sense than the Linux interface.
